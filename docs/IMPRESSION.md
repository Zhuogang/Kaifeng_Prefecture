# Direction: a painted impression of Kaifeng

This replaces the photoreal, historically strict city. `docs/ART_BIBLE.md` and `docs/CONTRACT.md` still apply where they don't conflict: Song architecture, tooling, gates. `main` keeps the earlier version.

**What it is.** A place you drift through, made of composed scenes that look like hand-painted backgrounds from a Chinese animated feature. Not a city to walk across. Beauty and composition come first. Geography and scale bend to serve the picture.

**Look targets:** `refs/style/c1.png` (宣德楼) and `refs/style/c2.png` (州桥). Study them: soft gouache with visible brushwork, simplified shapes, luminous golden light, real but soft light and shadow, cool violet-blue shadows against warm light, rich harmonious colour, atmospheric depth, foreground framing by branches.

**What stays strict.** Song architecture details, because they're cheap and they stop this becoming generic fantasy China: large brackets, deep gently curved eaves, 鸱尾 with 拒鹊子, flat timber gate lintels, a low beam-and-column 州桥, no yellow roofs, no Qing forms, no round arched gates.

**What's loose.** Real positions, distances, the 312 m avenue, the flat plain, the evidence tags. Scene-specific composition is now allowed and encouraged, including objects and dressing placed for a view.

**Assets.** The client allowed painted images for sky and far distance only (`assets/backdrops/`). Everything near stays in code.

**The main risk.** Real brushwork in moving 3D tends to look like a toon shader, or like a paint filter sliding over the image (the "shower door" effect). Everything must be judged in motion, not only as a still.
